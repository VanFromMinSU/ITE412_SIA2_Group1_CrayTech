## Project Summary

CrayTech is an integrated platform designed to support crayfish farming operations through Internet of Things (IoT) technology and e-commerce capabilities. The system aims to monitor important aquaculture conditions in real time while providing an online marketplace where customers can browse and purchase crayfish products from Mamen's Redclaw Aquafarm.

By combining environmental monitoring, farm management, and online selling into a single platform, CrayTech seeks to improve operational efficiency, support informed decision-making, and expand market reach for the aquafarm.
